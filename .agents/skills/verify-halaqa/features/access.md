# Access and delivery

Verify **Accounts**, **Students** and **History & corrections** share one Settings navigation row with a visible underline and `aria-current="page"` on the current link. Use the directional arrow key and Enter in English and Arabic. Reload Students and History, then use browser Back to restore the preceding section. At 320px, 390px and desktop widths, check that all three links fit on one row with 44px targets in both themes.

Use one browser for the administrator, an invited teacher and a student. Open **Profile → Sign in to another account**, cancel a failed login, then sign in and finish the invited user's setup. Select a saved account from Profile, verify the active role's controls, reload, and switch back. Check an adjacent open tab, an independent browser with no saved sessions, current-only sign-out and password/session expiry. Run `e2e/account-switching.spec.ts` and the disposable PostgreSQL suite `tests/device-accounts-integration.test.ts`. Keep traces off for real sessions.

In Settings, create a teacher with their name and phone number. Confirm the number directly with the recipient and click the confirmation button. Edit the number and check that creating the account requires another confirmation. Tab between fields to check visible focus. Retry after a lost response. Check that the private message wraps in Arabic at 320px and copies the phone sign-in link, verified number and generated password. Deny clipboard writes and verify manual copy selection. On leaving the screen, verify the message disappears. Sign in by phone as an invited teacher or student and reload the setup screen. Verify that the phone stays read-only, the new password is the sole editable field, and Save details and Sign out have space between them. Save a new password and verify the workspace opens in the original browser session. Edit the personal contact number and password afterwards and verify that profile settings remains available after reload. Run `e2e/invitations.spec.ts` and `e2e/invite-profile.spec.ts` on desktop and mobile in English and Arabic. The disposable PostgreSQL invitation test covers normalized duplicates, atomic provisioning, role boundaries, concurrent setup, continuity of the authenticated session, and revocation of the starter password and other sessions.

Sign in through the app. Both public entry points require sign-in to open the notebook. In English and Arabic at desktop and 320px, check the phone and password fields, the `09` and `+9639` hint and the full-width Sign in action in light and dark themes. Check a wrong-password retry and no horizontal overflow. Run `e2e/neon.spec.ts` for synthetic same-origin Auth/API sign-in, session restoration, account changes, failed and uncertain requests, retry, unavailable or corrupt pending storage, competing tabs and malformed snapshots. Check that a foreign batch appearing after page load blocks writes, a stale tab cannot retry a cleared batch, and an older client holding its account lock prevents a concurrent save. An unsuccessful sign-out must hide private records and offer another attempt. A confirmed sign-out returns home. Assert that failed storage or a held lock prevents unsafe additional writes and that retry retains event IDs. Run `e2e/roles.spec.ts` for denied access, role isolation and Administrator password reset. Confirm the acting Administrator cannot reset their own account.

Pending batches can contain private data. Web Locks and storage are required for safe live saves; reconcile uncertain batches before clearing storage. Synthetic tests do not prove live provider behavior.

Administrator entry and reload start on the dashboard with no account management. Open the profile avatar, then **Settings**. Verify both `view=settings` and the older `view=administration` link. Accounts initially shows compact rows, with no visible role fields and no acting Administrator card. Click an account summary and check that no outline surrounds the row. Tab to an account summary, check that **Manage** is underlined, press Enter to open it and Tab into the role field to see its focus ring. Switch between **Accounts** and **Students** and verify that only the selected section appears. The student table wrapper has a tinted background when focused by keyboard. A directory containing only the acting Administrator shows **No other accounts.** in English and Arabic.

Expand a Teacher account in **Settings → Accounts** in English and Arabic at desktop and phone widths. Check that the permissions selector is absent, role and expiry can be saved, and the form fits without horizontal overflow. Sign in with a previously restricted Teacher and verify writes for an active assigned student. Check the notebook response and direct Grades navigation for grade privacy.

Under **Manage**, enter or replace a teacher's phone number and use the confirmation button after asking the account holder. Verify the account summary shows the new number and sign-in works with it. Click **Reset password** and check that the copyable message contains the phone sign-in link, number and generated password. Copy it and compare the clipboard with the whole message. Dismiss the message and verify focus returns to Reset password. Check English and Arabic at desktop and phone widths. The disposable Railway and invitation tests verify authorization, old password and session revocation, successful sign-in with the generated password, and preserved invitation setup status.
