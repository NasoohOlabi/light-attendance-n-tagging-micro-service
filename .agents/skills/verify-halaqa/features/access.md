# Access and delivery

Use one browser for the administrator, an invited teacher and a student. Open **Profile → Sign in to another account**, cancel a failed login, then sign in and finish the invited user's setup. Select a saved account from Profile, verify the active role's controls, reload, and switch back. Check an adjacent open tab, an independent browser with no saved sessions, current-only sign-out and password/session expiry. Run `e2e/account-switching.spec.ts` and the disposable PostgreSQL suite `tests/device-accounts-integration.test.ts`. Keep traces off for real sessions.

In Settings, create one user with first and last names and an assigned role. Click the form background and check that the page has no outline, then Tab between fields and verify visible keyboard focus. Verify the editing screen has no spreadsheet field or preview action. Retry after a lost response. On the confirmation screen, check that the private message wraps in Arabic at 320px. Copy twice and compare the clipboard with the message, including the username sign-in link. Deny clipboard writes and verify the full message is selected with manual copy instructions. Return to Accounts and verify the message disappears. Follow username sign-in, required password replacement, phone and birth date, renewed login and later edits under Profile settings. Run `e2e/invitations.spec.ts` and `e2e/invite-profile.spec.ts` on desktop and mobile. `tests/invitations-integration.test.ts` exercises Better Auth sessions, atomic provisioning, role boundaries and rotation on a disposable migrated PostgreSQL database.

Sign in through the app or explore the sample. Run `e2e/neon.spec.ts` for synthetic same-origin Auth/API sign-in, session restoration, account changes, failed and uncertain requests, retry, unavailable or corrupt pending storage, competing tabs and malformed snapshots. Check that a foreign batch appearing after page load blocks writes, a stale tab cannot retry a cleared batch, and an older client holding its account lock prevents a concurrent save. An unsuccessful sign-out must hide private records and offer another attempt. A confirmed sign-out returns home. Assert that failed storage or a held lock prevents unsafe additional writes and that retry retains event IDs. Run `e2e/roles.spec.ts` for approval, denied access, role isolation and Administrator password reset; confirm the acting Administrator cannot reset their own account.

Pending batches can contain private data. Web Locks and storage are required for safe live saves; reconcile uncertain batches before clearing storage. Synthetic tests do not prove live provider behavior.

Administrator entry and reload start on the dashboard with no account management. Open the profile avatar, then **Settings**. Verify both `view=settings` and the older `view=administration` link. Accounts initially shows compact rows, with no visible role fields and no acting Administrator card. Click an account summary and check that no outline surrounds the row. Tab to an account summary, check that **Manage** is underlined, press Enter to open it and Tab into the role field to see its focus ring. Switch between **Accounts** and **Students** and verify that only the selected section appears. The student table wrapper has a tinted background when focused by keyboard. A directory containing only the acting Administrator shows **No other accounts.** in English and Arabic.

Expand a Teacher account in **Settings → Accounts** in English and Arabic at desktop and phone widths. Check that the permissions selector is absent, role and expiry can be saved, and the form fits without horizontal overflow. Sign in with a previously restricted Teacher and verify writes for an active assigned student. Check the notebook response and direct Grades navigation for grade privacy.

Under **Manage**, click **Reset password** and check that a copyable message contains the sign-in link, account identifier and generated password. Copy it and compare the clipboard with the whole message. Dismiss the message and verify focus returns to Reset password. Check English and Arabic at desktop and phone widths. The disposable Railway and invitation tests verify old password and session revocation, successful sign-in with the generated password, and preserved invitation setup status.

Follow a decision author's account-name link into Settings. Direct reload must retain the selected account; Back returns to Grades. Another account opens its management controls, while a specifically linked acting administrator shows read-only name/email without role, reset or disable controls. Ordinary Settings still omits self, and unknown account links show an unavailable message. Verify navigation sends no writes and fits desktop/320/390px English/Arabic in both palettes; Teacher/Student access remains denied.

Verify a single branded account header on notebook, Courses, Settings and Add student, plus the Student profile menu. An existing session photo renders as the avatar, with a fallback when unavailable. Open the profile menu by keyboard and pointer; check Escape restores trigger focus, outside clicks dismiss it, language switching preserves drafts/filters, and Sign out (Close session in samples) leaves the workspace. Settings remains Administrator-only. Check desktop and 320/390px English/Arabic layouts for touch targets, dropdown visibility and page overflow while keeping bottom page navigation usable.

Open **Profile settings** from the account menu for each authenticated role. Change the name and upload a synthetic PNG, JPEG or WebP image, save, reload and check the photo and name persist; remove the photo and save again. Check rejected oversized/invalid images and failed saves. Theme and language choices show their current selection with text and icons. `e2e/profile.spec.ts` uses synthetic sessions; `tests/profile.test.ts` covers the server validation boundary. Profile pictures are resized client-side before saving in the existing Auth user record. Live provider persistence remains a separate check.

Open **Add student** from the dashboard, save or cancel, and confirm Accounts/role controls are absent from the DOM. Verify the Settings directory and expanded editor at desktop/mobile widths, keyboard disclosure, Arabic RTL and no horizontal overflow. Teachers and Students cannot open Settings. Keep synthetic Settings overview/editor screenshots under ignored `.artifacts/verification/`.

Settings and Add student use the main navigation. Verify there is no separate Open notebook button in English or Arabic. From Settings, open Dashboard, Attendance, Courses and Students directly, then use browser Back to restore Settings. At 320px in English and Arabic, verify a single bounded navigation row with targets at least 44px wide and tall, and scroll the final Settings control above the fixed phone navigation. For app updates, run the real-worker case in `e2e/pwa.spec.ts`: replace the legacy cache-first worker, then change server HTML and use ordinary reloads to observe the new versions while another tab retains an unsaved note. Verify offline/stalled fallback, private request exclusions and preservation of unrelated scope caches. Update copy must ask users to save and refresh, without closing all windows.

During session and account restoration, verify the neutral workspace placeholder contains no sign-in, sample, sign-out or role controls. Hold the first notebook snapshot and confirm no zero counts or empty charts appear; check the selected query remains intact. Exercise session and account retry, including a late denial from an earlier timed-out account request, on desktop and mobile. Confirm Arabic RTL loading has no overflow. Refresh after loading must retain the visible snapshot.

With a selected course, keep the avatar centered beside the logo. Choose a course named with the wordmark and a number and check that its highlighted number sits beside the logo and opens the same dropdown with complete names. Check Arabic-Indic and Western digits. Other course names appear in full on their own row. Check that long English and Arabic names wrap at 320px, 390px, the desktop breakpoint and wide desktop. The avatar and course selector remain at least 44px in both dimensions, and the dropdown stays on screen. Run `e2e/course-preference.spec.ts` and the shared header geometry checks.

For MCP authorization, open the signed login and consent pages with synthetic accounts. Verify the active identity is visible, a saved account can be switched, another account can sign in by username or email, and allow/decline preserve the signed query. Check the sensitive read/write warning and both English and Arabic layouts at desktop and 320px. Synthetic endpoints do not prove live OAuth provider behavior.

For MCP invitations, verify `invite_accounts` returns a Wartaq link and a card resource without the username or password. Open the link in a signed-in browser and confirm no message loads until **Reveal invitation** is clicked. Verify the copyable message in English and Arabic on desktop and at 320px, then reload and try the spent link. Run `e2e/invitations.spec.ts` and the disposable PostgreSQL `tests/mcp-integration.test.ts` for the creating administrator, a different administrator, unauthenticated and foreign-origin requests, expiry, concurrent redemption, and sign-in with the delivered password.
