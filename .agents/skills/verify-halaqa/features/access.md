# Access and delivery

Sign in through the app or explore the sample. Run `e2e/neon.spec.ts` for synthetic same-origin Auth/API sign-in, session restoration, account changes, failed and uncertain requests, retry, unavailable or corrupt pending storage, competing tabs and malformed snapshots. Assert that failed storage or a held lock prevents unsafe additional writes and that retry retains event IDs. Run `e2e/roles.spec.ts` for approval, denied access, role isolation and Administrator password reset; confirm the acting Administrator cannot reset their own account.

Pending batches can contain private data. Web Locks and storage are required for safe live saves; reconcile uncertain batches before clearing storage. Synthetic tests do not prove live provider behavior.

Administrator entry and reload start on the dashboard with no account management. Open **Settings** using the gear; verify both `view=settings` and the older `view=administration` link. Accounts initially shows compact rows, with no visible role fields and no acting Administrator card. Focus an account summary and press Enter to open **Manage**, then verify role assignment and password mismatch validation. Switch between **Accounts** and **Students** and verify that only the selected section appears. A directory containing only the acting Administrator shows **No other accounts.** in English and Arabic.

Open **Add student** from the dashboard, save or cancel, and confirm Accounts/role controls are absent from the DOM. Verify the Settings directory and expanded editor at desktop/mobile widths, keyboard disclosure, Arabic RTL and no horizontal overflow. Teachers and Students cannot open Settings. Keep synthetic Settings overview/editor screenshots under ignored `.artifacts/verification/`.

Settings and Add student retain the notebook navigation. From Settings, open Dashboard, Attendance and Courses directly, then use browser Back to restore Settings. At 320px in English and Arabic, verify a single bounded navigation row with targets at least 44px wide and tall, and scroll the final Settings control above the fixed phone navigation. For waiting app updates, run the real-worker case in `e2e/pwa.spec.ts`: the localized notice appears without changing an unsaved note, including when another page opens after the worker is already waiting.
