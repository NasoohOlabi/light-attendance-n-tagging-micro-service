# Courses and enrollment

Use synthetic identities and records. On desktop and mobile, Courses defaults to the full saved list, including zero-enrollment courses after reload. Create/edit opens its own page with one heading; there is no generic notebook welcome/count or duplicated administrator identity. Keep each course row's actions visible at 320/390px in English/Arabic. Choose **Manage students**. Search the roster by name; assert there is no student selector or grade form on this screen. Toggle membership on/off and edit the independent course level. Verify new enrollment drafts use the roster level, an existing blank course level remains blank, reload retains membership and level, and changes/search do not erase grades. Retain distinct labelled columns and keyboard scrolling in English/Arabic at 320/390px.

Choose **Add new student to this course**. Verify the normal student form retains course context and a course-level field without grade inputs; cancel returns to course management without exposing accounts. Save must create one roster student and one active enrollment together, then return to that course. Exercise Back/forward and reload. A lost-response retry must not create duplicates; failed course/teacher validation must leave no orphan student.

Open the separate **Grades** destination, enter optional practical (عملي) and theory (نظري) scores including 0/100, save and reload. Grade entry must preserve membership and level; membership changes must preserve scores. Enroll one student in two courses, mark different statuses on the same date and switch courses without mixing records. After switching membership off, new attendance is denied while historical summary, corrections and grades remain available. Student responses stay private, and Teacher/Student management requests are denied. `e2e/courses.spec.ts` drives the synthetic UI; `tests/course-postgres.test.ts` and `tests/student-transfer-integration.test.ts` verify real SQL, atomic creation, repeatable migrations 005/006 and version 1–5 backups when disposable databases are configured.

Open **Students** and verify the separate Level cell follows the selected course enrollment, including a blank enrollment that must not inherit the roster level. Switching between two courses must never combine their levels in one cell. With no course selected, show the roster level. Check column labels, exact cells, reload and the student action on desktop and a bounded 320/390px English/Arabic table.

Close a course manually and reload: its status remains Closed, grades and membership remain available, and it is absent from notebook course selectors. Open a saved course history URL and verify history and corrections still work while new attendance is blocked. Reopen and verify attendance is available again. Check English and Arabic on desktop and mobile. The server must reject stale writes, accept exact retries, and deny status changes to teachers and students.

Verify Handbook received starts unchecked, is disabled until an enrollment exists, and persists independently across two courses, withdrawal/rejoining, level and grade changes, and reload. Include the optional profile fields in course-context student creation. Migration 007 is repeatable and backup version 6 retains these values.
