# Assigned roster

Administrators maintain the complete student register, including historical students without a teacher assignment. Assignment controls Teacher access; it does not determine whether a student record is retained. Unassigned students display **Unassigned** in Settings and can be edited, kept inactive or assigned to an approved active Teacher later. Existing provisional or inactive assignments may be preserved while editing a profile; they cannot be newly assigned. Ignore the unmaintained old/former source flag and preserve stable IDs on roster updates. Keep real data outside Git and use the private CLI in [Railway setup](railway-setup.md) when importing.

The server checks the authenticated role, active state, expiry, Teacher access mode and current assignment. Teachers see active assigned students and can submit student-specific attendance and notes according to their mode. Reassignment changes staff access to the student's history. Administrators have the full notebook; Students receive only their own projected attendance. Signup alone grants no access. See [roles](roles.md).

Administrator student forms store optional WhatsApp number, general location and birth year (year only), alongside the existing roster level. Blank values clear these fields; legacy updates that omit them preserve them. Read/write teachers can update the phone number of their own active assigned students from the offline notebook. A concurrent phone edit asks the teacher to review both numbers. The other profile fields and assignments remain administrator-managed. Student attendance responses omit staff contact details and notes.

The notebook Students table puts **First name**, **Last name**, **Phone** and **Attendance** first. Phone remains isolated left-to-right; missing values show localized **Not provided**. Staff phone fields in the list, student information and Administrator register are underlined call links with 44px touch targets. Tapping opens the device dialer, where the user can call or use its contact actions. Display adds `+` to bare 11–15 digit numbers without a leading zero and converts long `00` international prefixes to `+`; existing `+` and local leading zeros are preserved. Shorter numbers keep their original form, and invalid values remain plain text. This length heuristic cannot identify every country or validate a subscriber. Stored values, edit fields, sorting and filtering remain unchanged. Attendance shows Present divided by effective recorded sessions, rounded to one decimal, alongside a labelled session count. Unmarked days are excluded; no records shows **Not recorded**. All students summarizes every accessible course and unscoped attendance; selecting a course scopes these figures to that student's enrollment. Explicit name parts are preserved; legacy unsplit names stay intact without guessing a surname.

**Open student** opens a separate, reloadable **Student information** page with first/last names, the prominent phone field, attendance totals (Present, Absent, Excused, recorded sessions and percentage), courses and effective attendance history. A separate **Take attendance** action appears only when the student and chosen attendance context are eligible. Back returns to the list; browser Back/reload preserve the selected record and course. Inactive historical students remain readable by Administrators with their recorded history but cannot receive new attendance or notes. Teachers receive only their active assigned student profiles; the Student portal continues to expose only its own attendance projection, without these staff contact fields.

Spreadsheet recovery respects explicit phone number formats: numeric cells with zero placeholders retain their displayed leading zeros. It does not invent country prefixes, clear an owner's changed contact value or create phone numbers where the source is blank. Keep the workbook, source mapping and repair backup private.

Settings lists active and inactive students. In the Administrator notebook, **Students** defaults to **All students** and shows the complete register, including inactive and unassigned historical students. The count includes this full register. **Find a student** supports partial names and common typos, and handles Arabic alif variants and diacritics. For example, searching `ابرا` finds a student whose family name is `إبراهيم`. Search works within the selected group and course. Teachers' All students remains limited to their active assigned students. Current attendance and dashboard use active students; course history retains its existing membership scope. Historical students can remain inactive without being deleted or being given invented attendance, enrollments or teacher identities. Recover explicit source name parts and contact details when present; derive only the birth year from a dated birth field, and leave unavailable information blank.

The labelled **Courses** column shows each currently enrolled course as a separate named badge, independently of the roster level or selected course's enrollment level. Every name remains available when a student has several courses; no active enrollments displays localized **None**. Closed courses remain listed while membership is active; withdrawn memberships are excluded. Wide layouts show the course names directly; narrow phones provide an expandable labelled course list. Entering Students from another destination returns to All students, and an explicit course filter survives reload.

Notebook Students and attendance tables give the remaining width to student names. Level, status and action columns have compact, stable widths, with centered headers and values. Text direction is isolated inside cells so Arabic, Latin names and short level values stay aligned. On compact screens, each notebook Students record starts with a full-name button that opens Student information. Phone, attendance, level, courses and status stay labelled beneath the name. Attendance controls span the record width.

The notebook Students heading has Sort and Filter menus for all seven fields. The wide table also offers sortable column headers. Sort cycles ascending, descending, and original order; empty values sort last. The clear control resets the table's sorting and filters. Free-text filters support fuzzy matching. Status choices use exact matching, while structured values such as phone numbers and rounded attendance percentages use literal text matching. Table state is kept in the URL for navigation. On attendance, bulk marking affects only the rows matching all current filters; drafts for hidden students remain available.

Sort headings use spaced chevrons that appear on hover or keyboard focus. Touch devices without hover keep the chevrons visible. The heading retains its pointer cursor, touch target and accessible sort state.
