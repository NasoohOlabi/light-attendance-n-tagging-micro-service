# Courses and enrollment

Administrators open **Courses** to see every saved course, including courses with no students. The default is the list; **Create course** opens a separate page and returns to the list after saving. Create/edit a course, **Manage students**, and **Grades** are separate destinations with individual page headings and the notebook navigation retained. Course pages omit the generic notebook introduction and unrelated student count. The localized account role appears once in the header. Choose a course from its row rather than combining course/student selectors and scores in one form.

**Manage students** searches the existing roster by name. Each student has separate identity, current roster level, course level and enrollment columns. The switch enrolls or withdraws that student without deleting their enrollment, history or grades. A new enrollment starts with the roster level as an editable draft; an existing course level remains independent, including a deliberately blank level. An administrator can choose the teacher for this enrollment in **Manage students**. Saving the level and teacher preserves both exam scores. The same student can have different teachers in different courses. Course teacher assignments do not grant notebook access. Switching off blocks new attendance for that enrollment on the server.

**Add new student to this course** opens the normal student form with the course context and a course-level field. Saving creates the roster entry and active enrollment in one transaction, then returns to managing that course. Cancel returns without creating either. Stable IDs make a retry safe if a response is lost. The ordinary dashboard Add student flow remains separate from courses.

Enter practical (عملي) and theory (نظري) scores later on **Grades**, not during enrollment or student creation. Scores are optional numbers from 0 through 100, including zero. Saving grades changes only scores; it preserves membership and level. A student can belong to several courses with one stable enrollment per student/course pair.

Select a course in the notebook to mark its enrolled students and see that course's attendance, summary, dashboard and attendance history. The previous/no-course view shows historical unscoped records; every new attendance mark requires an active enrollment in an open course. Notebook note events remain student-scoped; performance notes on Grades belong to the enrollment. Existing attendance is not assigned to any course automatically. A student account receives only its own projected attendance with course names, never notes or raw events. Teachers see enrollments for their assigned active students, subject to their existing access and expiry restrictions.

**Summary** shows all recorded dates for the selected course and every student available in that course's authorized history, including inactive memberships. Course selection is its only filter; saved date ranges, student search, group and student filters from other pages do not narrow these totals. Student note counts cover all dates and remain student-scoped. **Previous / no course** summarizes unscoped attendance. Open a student's note dialog from their Attendance row or use History & corrections to review earlier records.

**Absences by session** ranks all recorded course dates by the number of explicit absences, newest first when tied. Each date shows recorded and excused counts for context. Expand a date to see each recorded student and mark. Five dates appear initially, with the rest available below. The table retains each student's exact totals.

On **Dashboard**, the session date sets the end of a six-recorded-session window. **Students to follow up** lists current students with a recorded absence in that window, most absences first. The dated strip distinguishes Present, Absent, Excused and Unmarked. Open a name for contact details and attendance history. Take attendance opens the selected course and date. The marked and unmarked counts use the current roster.

Administrators also see **Course result readiness** for the selected course. **Needs practice** lists current students whose saved practical or theory score falls below that course's corresponding minimum. **Missing evidence** identifies students missing either score or any recorded course attendance. These counts use all saved course data independently of the dashboard date. A zero score counts as recorded. A result override preserves the underlying scores. Open Grades to work on that course.

From **Summary**, choose **Export attendance chart** to compare the students in the selected course across all recorded dates. The bars run from highest attendance to lowest. Each percentage includes the number present out of recorded sessions (Present, Absent and Excused). Students with no recorded sessions appear last with **No records**.

For a conversation with one student, use **Highlight one student by name**. Their classmates keep numbered labels. **Show all names** includes the whole group's names in the image. Check the preview, then download the PNG or use **Share image** on a device that supports sharing image files. Large groups span multiple images. Choose a single page to send or use **Download all images (ZIP)** to save the whole group. Images are generated on your device and contain attendance only.

The notebook **Students** list and the **Attendance** roster use the same course-level rule and keep student identity separate from the labelled Level column. With a course selected, Level shows only that student's level in that enrollment; a blank level displays an em dash. Attendance opens on the enrolled student list, ordered by name. The Teacher column shows the teacher on that course enrollment. Switching courses can show a different level for the same student. In **Previous / no course**, Level shows the existing roster level (the roster group value). Course levels never fall back to that roster value or to another enrollment. Roster group filters on other pages do not narrow Attendance.

For Administrators, choose **All students** in **Students** to show the complete register, including inactive historical and unassigned students. Students initially uses the chosen default course, or All students when there is no default. Its separate **Courses** column lists every active enrollment's course name as a badge, including closed courses; withdrawn memberships are omitted and no enrollment displays localized None. The names stay separate from roster and enrollment levels. This read view leaves active status unchanged; inactive students remain ineligible for new attendance and notes, and current course/attendance/Grades views retain their existing scope. Teachers continue to see only their active assigned students. Other notebook tabs retain **Previous / no course** for historical unscoped attendance.

Inactive enrollments remain available for historical attendance, corrections and grades. Current attendance and current enrolled-roster/dashboard counts exclude them. Teacher assignment and Student privacy restrictions still apply.

Grades omits the redundant Enrolled column; use Manage students to inspect or change current membership. Historical grades remain visible after withdrawal. Across the notebook, whole-number numeric levels display without a decimal suffix (`1.0` becomes `1`). Named levels and meaningful fractional values remain intact, blank course levels stay blank, and stored values are preserved.

Level columns follow the page language. Values align right in Arabic and left in English, including numeric levels and names written in another script.

Migration `neon/005-active-enrollments.sql` adds an active membership flag, defaulting existing enrollments to active, without deleting data. Backup versions 4 and later preserve that flag; versions 1–3 remain supported without silently hiding inactive memberships. Test against a disposable database first; synthetic tests do not verify the live provider.

Administrators can manually close and reopen a course from the course list. Manual closure can proceed with incomplete grades. Closed courses remain in the management list. Notebook selectors omit them unless the course is the saved default or the course currently being viewed; a previously saved course URL can still show history and corrections, while new attendance is rejected. Reopening permits attendance again. Notes remain student-scoped.

Closing from Courses or Grades updates the roster level for active students with an active enrollment, a saved effective Pass and a whole-number course level. Passing at course level 1 sets a lower or blank roster level to 2, and passing at level 2 sets it to 3. The result uses saved scores, projected attendance and the latest saved override. Higher roster levels and custom labels are preserved. The closed course keeps each student's original course level, grades and history. Retrying closure, or reopening and closing again with the same course level, never adds a second level. Previously closed courses receive no automatic backfill.

Apply `bun run db:migrate` through `neon/006-closed-courses.sql` before deployment. Existing and new courses default to open. Version 5 backups preserve course status; older backups restore courses as open and cannot compare successfully with closed courses. Test against a disposable database first.

Manage students records **Handbook received** independently for each existing enrollment. It defaults to false and survives withdrawal, re-enrollment, level and grade changes.

Course rows offer **Make current**, **Manage students**, **Grades**, **History**, **Edit**, and **Close course** or **Reopen course**. The actions appear as a row of named icons when the course list is at least 48rem wide. Narrower lists use a three-dot menu with labeled, touch-sized controls. The menu closes on Escape, an outside click, or selection. The current course carries a text label under its name.

On Grades, each course has independent minimum attendance (default 70%), practical passing grade (default 80), and theory passing grade (default 80). Both saved scores must meet their own threshold and attendance must meet its threshold. Attendance is Present divided by Present + Absent + Excused; unmarked days are excluded, and no records yield an unavailable attendance and pending result. Corrections and legacy early/late marks follow the normal event projection. Save thresholds and each row separately. The Grades page enables Close course only after every active enrollment has both grades saved and all grade and threshold drafts are saved. Inactive memberships remain visible and do not prevent closure. Manual close/reopen on the course list remains independent of grading.

Migration `neon/008-course-grading.sql` adds the course thresholds. Version 7 backups preserve them; older backups restore defaults and comparison rejects non-default target thresholds.

Each Grades row has a labeled **Save grades** button. The three-dot menu beside it opens secondary actions. Both controls sit beside the result on phones, on the left in Arabic and on the right in English. They have touch-sized targets on phones and desktop.

Table headings provide quick single-column sorting. Hold Shift while clicking another heading to add a secondary sort. A small Sort icon opens configuration in a popup; the table itself shows no configuration rows. Inside Sort, use **Add column**, drag a column pill's grip to change priority, toggle its direction icon, or remove it. Priority 1 is applied first; later pills break ties. Keyboard users can grab a grip with Space, reorder with arrow keys, drop with Space or cancel with Escape. **Reset sort** preserves filters. On tables that support filtering, the separate Filter icon opens column selection and filter editing; text matches contained text, choice fields match exactly, and filters combine. Sort order and filters survive reload in the view URL. Popups close with their close control, Escape or an outside click.

Grades includes optional multiline enrollment notes, saved with each row. Notes belong to that student and course, survive membership and grade changes, and are visible only to authorized staff; student attendance responses exclude them. Migration 010 adds the field with an empty default. Version 9 backups preserve it; older backups cannot compare against nonempty enrollment notes.

Grades opens directly on existing student records. Its Sort icon sits beside the page title, with no separate configuration row or Filter icon. Use the existing Courses navigation to return to the saved course list; Grades has no duplicate Back button. Saved Grades URL filters are ignored; heading sorting and the sort popup remain available. Edit scores and notes on each student's row. Student creation and membership remain on their separate screens. Other tables keep their filters inside the Filter popup.

Saved attendance and scores show green when they meet their saved thresholds and red when below them. These values identify failing criteria directly; Result does not repeat written failure explanations. Rows are shaded by the effective Pass or Fail result. Pending calculated rows stay neutral unless a saved override determines the effective result; a changed score stays neutral until saved, and all individual indicators stay neutral while threshold drafts differ from saved values. Other unchanged saved values can still be colored while a row is pending.

Administrators choose **Override result** from the student's three-dot menu. Choose Pass, Fail or Restore calculated result and enter a required nonblank note, then save the decision. Decision history shows the newest decision first, with its result, readable local date/time and full multiline reason. The administrator's account name links to their account in Settings; a blank name uses their email, and an unavailable account has a localized label without a dead link. Account IDs remain stored in the audit history but are not displayed. Cancel or Escape closes the modal without submitting and returns focus to the menu trigger. A failed save keeps the modal and note available for retry. Each decision is saved with the authenticated administrator and server time in an append-only enrollment history. The compact Result column retains calculated and effective results. Teachers and students do not receive decision notes or history. Decisions do not change scores, attendance, or closure rules.

Decision history resolves saved author IDs to their current profile names, so existing decisions reflect profile name changes. Apply migration `neon/011-grade-decisions.sql` before deployment. Version 10 backups preserve decision history; older backups cannot compare against a target with decisions.

The Courses and Manage students titles share the Sort and Filter toolbar used throughout the notebook. Grades shows Sort beside its title and Excel export. Grades uses a bounded scrolling table on desktop, with opaque sticky column headings. Focus the Course grades region to scroll it with the keyboard. On phones, Manage students and Grades show labelled records and use normal page scrolling. The compact Courses list shows aligned column headings.

Stacked grade records have consistent inset padding and separation. Theory and Practical inputs fill their paired columns; Notes, Result and Actions each span the record width. Labels and touch-sized controls remain inside the record in both languages and palettes.

From **Grades**, administrators can open **Export Excel**, choose a report and select **Download Excel**. On phones the download icon opens the same options.

| Report                   | Default contents                                                                                |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Full workbook            | Course status and thresholds, all memberships, saved exam scores, attendance totals and results |
| Grades                   | Student names, saved theory and practical scores, and effective results                         |
| Student details          | Names, course level and enrollment status                                                       |
| Daily attendance records | One row per recorded student and date, showing the effective mark after corrections             |
| Exam participants        | Students with a saved theory or practical score, including a score of zero                      |

Open **Advanced options** to pick columns and filter by student, course level, enrollment status, recorded exam scores or saved result. Daily attendance also supports inclusive dates and attendance status. For example, choose **Daily attendance records**, select **Absent**, then set the date range to download absences for that period. The matching-row count updates before downloading. Contact fields are available by explicit column selection.

Course downloads include withdrawn memberships and inactive roster students by default. Grade results reflect saved administrator decisions and full-course attendance. Missing scores stay blank. Exam participation is inferred from saved scores because the course has no separate exam attendance register. Notes and decision history are excluded. Save any grade or threshold edits before exporting. Grades page sorting and filters do not change export selections.

Light and dark appearance use matching readable red/green grade indicators and row tints. Color meanings are the same in both palettes.

Result sorting uses the effective result, including saved administrator decisions, and ranks pending, fail, then pass in ascending order, and reverses that order descending. Within each status, it sorts by the arithmetic mean of the saved theory and practical scores; pending rows missing either score precede completed-score pending rows in ascending order. Unsaved drafts retain their calculated pending status; an explicit administrator decision remains effective. Sorting does not save changes.

Choose **Default course** on Attendance. Administrators can also choose **Make current** from an open course's row actions. The choice is stored in this browser for each signed-in account and can be changed at any time. Multiple courses may remain open simultaneously. Notebook navigation uses the default, including after Settings or course management. Students initially uses the default but its course and All students filters are temporary and survive reload and Back without changing it. Dashboard and Summary use the selected course without a picker. A course in a direct link or a course History action shows that course without changing the default. Unavailable saved courses fall back to no course. Choose **Previous / no course** on Attendance, then open Summary for historical unscoped attendance. The header shows the selected course beside the logo. A name consisting of the Arabic brand and a number is shortened to the number, with the full name available to assistive technology.
